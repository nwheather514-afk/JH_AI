        Frequently asked questions

        What is AI security?

        It's the set of rules, settings, and habits that keep company and customer information safe when your team uses AI tools: which tools are approved, what may be entered, who can sign in, and what happens when something goes wrong.

        What does a managed AI security service do?

        We write and put those rules in place, set up the accounts and settings, train your team, and re-check every month so the protection doesn't fade.

        Why does it matter for a small business?

        A small team has fewer safeguards and fewer people to catch mistakes, so one careless paste can reach customers' information. A few simple rules go a long way.

        What happens in a consultation?

        In a call of about 10 minutes we hear about your situation and your results, then outline what to fix first and how we can help. Whether to go further is entirely up to you.

        How are the scope and price of the managed service decided?

        They depend on your company's size and your results. In the consultation we pick only the items you really need and give you a quote.

        Who can see my results?

        Until you sign up or request a consultation, your answers are not saved on our server. If you request a consultation, your score and answers go to the person handling it.

        Is it really free?

        The self-check and the report are free. Requesting a consultation does not obligate you to buy anything.

        Do I need to know IT to answer?

        No. The questions are written in everyday language, and you choose Yes / Partly / No. If you're not sure, answer No. That itself is a sign the check-up is worth doing.

        What information is stored?

        Only your email, company name, employee count (optional), and your answers. There is no field for customer information, documents, or AI conversations.

        Does a low score mean there's a big problem?

        Not necessarily. The score shows how many rules and settings are in place, and a one-page policy plus a few settings can often raise it noticeably. You can re-take the check to see the change.

        Is this legal advice?

        No. It's a self-assessment based on general principles of public frameworks (NIST AI RMF, NIST CSF). Confirm your legal obligations with an attorney or your trade association.
